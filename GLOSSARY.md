# PACCO Infrastructure Audit

PACCO tracks infrastructure audit transactions and their movement through review, suspension, and certification.

## Language

**Movement history**

The record of intake and status or lifecycle movements visible to administrative staff.

**Voided transaction**

A duplicate transaction removed from the active audit queue whose audit status and recorded history are retained.

**Actual action date**

The date on which an intake or audit action occurred, as declared by the person who records it.

**Recording timestamp**

The system time at which an intake or audit action was accepted into the shared record.
